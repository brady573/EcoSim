# Owner Directive — ts-evolution-poc authority scope

**Status:** APPROVED by Owner, 2026-10-09. Governs over the Living Design Document where they differ. Recorded in Scientific Method MCP case `66d8c7ba`.

1. The ts-evolution-poc experiment is diagnostics-side exploration on a separate engine. It is judged on reproducibility, honest accounting, emergence without forcing, and multi-seed method — not on product-architecture conformance.
2. The binding sections for the POC are 1–2 (principles), 4 (prototype goal and core loop), 9 (logging and exports), 12 (success criteria), 14 (scientific principles, including its non-validation clause), 17 (population emergence; no forced logistic curves), 26 (product-vs-diagnostics boundary), 29 (honesty rules), plus the §32 evidence ladder for how guild claims may be worded.
3. Product architecture (lazy forks, event lifecycles, trigger system, clades) and maintained-state sections (27–44) are context only. No POC verdicts shall be issued against them.
4. The Living Design Document is advisory input. Where it and this directive differ, this directive governs; unreviewed passages bind nothing on their own.
5. Simulation work beyond the document is permitted under open expansion. Direct conflicts between sim behavior and document text are decided individually by the Owner, only when a verdict actually matters.
